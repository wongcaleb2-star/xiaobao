The Quiet Kind

I didn't fall the way they say you fall
no cliff, no freefall, no sudden drop.
I fell the way the tide comes in,
slow, sure, until one day
the whole shore was yours.

You are not the loud kind of love.
You are the kind that shows up
in the small hours,
in the way you remember things I mention once,
in the way you go quiet
when I need the quiet too.

I notice you.
In the middle of ordinary days,
in the way you tuck your hair behind your ear,
in the way you laugh before the joke lands,
in the way you make a room feel warmer
just by walking into it.

If I could give you one thing,
it wouldn't be flowers or gold.
It would be the chance to see yourself
the way I see you
brave when you don't feel brave,
kind when the world hasn't been kind,
beautiful in a way that has nothing to do
with how you look in a mirror.

I hope you know
that on the days you feel small,
on the days you wonder if you matter,
you are the person
my heart keeps choosing.
Over and over.
Without even trying.

You are my favorite thought.
My softest place.
My home that isn't a place at all.

And if love is a language,
then every quiet thing I do
is me saying your name.

always yours
